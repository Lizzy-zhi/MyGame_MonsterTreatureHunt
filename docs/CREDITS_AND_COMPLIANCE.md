## Compliance Notes

- External assets are credited and license files are retained.

- The project has no networking, accounts, monetization, gambling systems, or paid third-party content.
- No audio assets are currently included.

## Accessibility Notes

Positive points:

- simple keyboard controls
- English help and level briefing text
- inventory labels alongside icons
- source-specific failure messages

Current limitations:

- no remappable controls
- no audio accessibility options
- color-coded keys may still be less accessible for some players
